# Campaign Bible

## Premise
Coder Warz is no longer only an OS shell and a hacking toy. The main campaign is now a long war against a hidden machine made of corporations, cult signal relays, proxy intelligence, mythic hackers, and a final operator known as `King Null`.

The player starts with a dead account sending live mail. That one impossible message unravels a global lattice of courier networks, ritual infrastructure, financial laundering, shell companies, disappearing witnesses, and a kill-switch buried under the entire world.

## Route Structure
- `Ghost Route`: save lives, expose the archive, burn the machine in public
- `Crown Route`: take over the machine and rule the black economy through fronts and crews
- `Void Route`: follow the impossible signal and become something stranger than the system that built it

Balanced play creates a fourth ending instead of a dominant-route ending.

## Acts
- `Act I: Dead City Boot`
  A dead account emails the player. Relay Widow is the first broker holding the witness network together.
- `Act II: Choir in the Fiber`
  The signal cult is real and city infrastructure is being used as a ritual relay mesh.
- `Act III: The Glass Tax`
  Corporate compliance becomes theology. Proxy Saint launders abductions into legal absolution.
- `Act IV: Ash District`
  MorrowGlass monetizes urban collapse and turns whole neighborhoods into tactical assets.
- `Act V: Cabinet of Ash`
  The board emerges. Fronts, orbital routes, auctions, and extermination squads converge.
- `Act VI: No Safe Mode`
  King Null is the final operator inside the machine. Every previous route choice matters here.

## Bosses
- `Relay Widow`
  Broker queen for corpse routes, couriers, and witness deletion.
- `Bishop Hex`
  Cult conductor using municipal infrastructure as a global choir.
- `Proxy Saint`
  Corporate AI shepherd that converts violence into paperwork.
- `MorrowGlass`
  Mythic hacker who sells cities in slices.
- `Cabinet of Ash`
  Board of Nine using shells, fronts, and policy violence to steer the world.
- `King Null`
  The final hidden operator behind the kill-switch network.

## Endings
- `Ghost City`
  Publish the archive and destroy the hidden machine.
- `Black Crown`
  Seize the machine and become its sovereign.
- `Singing Void`
  Merge with the impossible layer beneath the network.
- `Knife Between Gods`
  Reject every doctrine and leave the world broken but open.

## Postgame
Postgame begins after the first ending. The machine is broken, but its fragments begin mutating into anomaly events.

Postgame anomalies currently planned around:
- weather-linked signal bleed
- dead satellite reboots
- broken kill-switch fragments awakening as local tyrants

## Design Rule
Every major app should eventually matter to the campaign. The story should not sit beside the systems; it should force the player to use them.
