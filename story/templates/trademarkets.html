Game Trading Markets

This document outlines various markets available for player interaction, enabling trading gameplay across different asset classes. Each market and asset has specific characteristics influencing prices, risks, and accessibility.

Commodities Market

Gold

  • Market Type: Global Commodity
  • Volatility: Low (Stable)
  • Liquidity: High
  • Base Price (per ounce): $1,800
  • Factors Influencing Price: Global economic stability, geopolitical tensions, major financial news.
  • Trading Mechanics: Spot Buy/Sell, Futures.
  • Access Requirements: Standard brokerage account (e.g., via OffChainBank).
  • Risk Factors: Minor market fluctuations, storage costs.
  • Location: Global Exchange

Lithium

  • Market Type: Industrial Commodity
  • Volatility: Medium
  • Liquidity: Medium
  • Base Price (per kg): $70
  • Factors Influencing Price: Demand for [AutoBrand] EVs, tech product launches, mining supply news, environmental policies.
  • Trading Mechanics: Spot Buy/Sell, Supply Contracts.
  • Access Requirements: Specialized industrial broker.
  • Risk Factors: Supply chain disruptions, technological shifts.
  • Location: Global Industrial Exchange

Silicon

  • Market Type: Industrial Commodity
  • Volatility: Medium-Low
  • Liquidity: Medium
  • Base Price (per kg): $25
  • Factors Influencing Price: Chip manufacturing demand, [TechBrand] product cycles, geopolitical trade relations.
  • Trading Mechanics: Spot Buy/Sell, Bulk Contracts.
  • Access Requirements: Industrial procurement license.
  • Risk Factors: Manufacturing bottlenecks, trade tariffs.
  • Location: Global Industrial Exchange

Rare Earths

  • Market Type: Strategic Commodity
  • Volatility: High
  • Liquidity: Low
  • Base Price (per gram, blended): $10
  • Factors Influencing Price: Geopolitical supply, [HardwareBrand] component manufacturing, military tech advancements.
  • Trading Mechanics: Futures, Illicit Procurement.
  • Access Requirements: High-level industrial contracts, or dark web connections.
  • Risk Factors: Extreme supply volatility, ethical sourcing concerns, high black market markups.
  • Location: Global Strategic Materials Exchange / Dark Web

Lumber

  • Market Type: Renewable Commodity
  • Volatility: Medium
  • Liquidity: Medium
  • Base Price (per board foot): $4
  • Factors Influencing Price: Housing market demand, environmental regulations, weather events in forested regions.
  • Trading Mechanics: Spot Buy/Sell, Bulk Orders.
  • Access Requirements: Construction supply network.
  • Risk Factors: Seasonal fluctuations, natural disasters.
  • Location: Local / Regional Markets

In-Game Brands Market

[Pear] Products (Secondary Market)

  • Market Type: Consumer Electronics
  • Volatility: Medium
  • Liquidity: Low
  • Base Price: Varies per product
  • Factors Influencing Price: New product launches, recalls, repair costs, rarity of specific models.
  • Trading Mechanics: Buy/Sell (Physical or Digital Contracts).

  • Factors Influencing Price: New OS updates, security vulnerabilities, enterprise demand.

Digital Assets Market

Raw Data Streams

  • Market Type: Digital Information
  • Volatility: High
  • Liquidity: Medium
  • Base Price (per MB): Varies widely ($0.01 - $100+)
  • Factors Influencing Price: Origin (corporate, government), sensitivity, exclusivity, market demand (e.g., for specific research, blackmail).
  • Trading Mechanics: Buy/Sell (Data packets), Data Futures.
  • Access Requirements: Dark Web Marketplaces, direct contact with data brokers.
  • Risk Factors: Legal risks, traceback, data corruption, fake data.
  • Location: Dark Web / Encrypted Networks

Zero-Day Exploits

  • Market Type: Digital Weaponry / Vulnerabilities
  • Volatility: Extreme
  • Liquidity: Very Low (Highly specialized)
  • Base Price: $50,000 - $5,000,000+
  • Factors Influencing Price: Target system prevalence, exploit reliability, stealth, political climate.
  • Trading Mechanics: Auction, Direct Sale (Highly Confidential).
  • Access Requirements: Elite hacker networks, intelligence agencies, deep dark web.
  • Risk Factors: Extreme legal repercussions, immediate patch/obsolescence, double-crossing.
  • Location: Deep Dark Web / Private Secure Channels

Illicit Goods Market

Forged Identities

  • Market Type: Illicit Services
  • Volatility: Medium
  • Liquidity: Low
  • Base Price: $500 - $10,000 (depending on quality/detail)
  • Factors Influencing Price: Level of verification, target country, associated digital footprints.
  • Trading Mechanics: Direct Purchase (via encrypted comms).
  • Access Requirements: Trusted dark web contacts, specific mission unlocks.
  • Risk Factors: Legal risks, compromised identities, identity theft.
  • Location: Dark Web Marketplaces
