Apps That Make, Need, or Have Accounts

  • Internet (Browser): Used to access websites that require accounts.
  • App Store: Requires a user account for managing purchases, downloads, and app installations.
  • Mail Client: Handles emails, which are account-based.
  • OffChainBank: Manages banking and transfers, requiring bank accounts.
  • Chatroom: Typically requires user accounts or handles for identification.
  • Hacker Simulator: The core gameplay involves interacting with simulated systems that have accounts.
  • My Network Places / Network: Used to explore networks where other resources might require authentication/accounts.
  • Overclocker: Could be tied to a system profile or user account on the PC.
  • Notes: Can store sensitive account information.
