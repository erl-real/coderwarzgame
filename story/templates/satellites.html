Game Satellites and Space Assets

This document details the various orbital and space-based assets in the game, including their functions, connectivity, and stats relevant for hacking, data operations, and infrastructure development.

Comms Sat: EchoNet-01

  • Type: Communication Satellite
  • Description: A standard low-earth orbit communications satellite, providing basic data relay.
  • Orbital Parameters: Low Earth Orbit (LEO)
  • Connected Servers: Manhattan Digital Fortress (New York), Thames Node Exchange (London), Iron Curtain Cloud (Berlin)
  • Visibility Schedule:
    • Manhattan Digital Fortress: Daily 08:00-09:00 UTC, 20:00-21:00 UTC
    • Thames Node Exchange: Daily 10:00-11:00 UTC, 22:00-23:00 UTC
    • Iron Curtain Cloud: Daily 11:30-12:30 UTC, 23:30-00:30 UTC
  • Relay Capability: Yes (Max 2 hops)
  • Hacking Stats:
    • Difficulty: Easy
    • Reward (Data): Low-value Comms Data (5-15 MB)
    • Speed Boost/Optimization: +10% Network Speed for connected ground servers
    • Intercept Chance: 70% success
    • Traceback Risk: Low (5%)
    • Special Abilities: Data bouncing, basic comms relay.
  • Deployment/Acquisition Cost: N/A (Part of default infrastructure)
